# Content — Supporting Pages

Content specs for non-service pages. No design.

---

## Home (`/`)
**Goal:** orient, route to the right service, build trust, capture high-intent enquiries.

Content order:
1. One-line value statement: what the business does (IPR registration services in India) and who it's for.
2. Primary action (Start Enquiry).
3. The four services as clear entry points — each with a one-line description and a link to its page (Trademark, Copyright, Patent, Privacy Policy).
4. "Which service do I need?" mapping (asset type → service).
5. How it works in brief (3–4 steps: enquire → we assess → we file → you're protected), link to full How It Works.
6. Trust strip: experience/credentials, honest no-guarantee statement, what happens after you enquire.
7. Short FAQ (cross-service) + link to full FAQ.
8. Primary action (repeat).

Must link directly to all four service pages.

---

## Services Hub (`/services`)
**Goal:** gateway to the four categories + help unsure users choose.

Content:
1. Short intro to the range of IPR services.
2. The four services, each with a summary and link.
3. Full "Which service do I need?" decision guide (from 02-NAVIGATION.md).
4. Note that some assets (e.g. logos) may need more than one service.
5. Primary action.

---

## How It Works (`/how-it-works`)
**Goal:** demystify the generic engagement journey.

Content:
1. The universal journey: Enquiry → Consultation/assessment → Document collection → Filing → Government process (examination/objection/etc.) → Outcome/certificate.
2. What the business does vs what the client provides at each stage.
3. Communication cadence and status tracking.
4. Honest note: government stages are outside anyone's control; no outcome guaranteed.
5. Links to each service's detailed process.
6. Primary action.

---

## Pricing (`/pricing`)
**Goal:** transparent cost drivers without over-promising a single figure.

Content:
1. Explanation that total cost = government fees + professional/service fees, and what drives each.
2. Per-service cost drivers:
   - Trademark: number of classes; entity size (₹4,500 vs ₹9,000 per class govt fee); objection/hearing/opposition scope.
   - Copyright: work category; published vs unpublished; additional documents for software/artistic works (₹500–₹5,000 range per work).
   - Patent: provisional vs complete; specification complexity; drawings; PCT/convention routes.
   - Privacy Policy: [OWNER TO SUPPLY].
3. Clear statement that government fees are set by the authorities and subject to change (verify-before-launch).
4. Primary action (get a precise quote via enquiry).

---

## About (`/about`)
**Goal:** establish credibility and trust.

Content:
1. Who the business is and its focus on IPR services.
2. Experience, credentials, and approach.
3. Why choose this business (transparency, process clarity, support through disputes).
4. Consistent entity name (GEO/SEO benefit).
5. Primary action.

---

## Contact / Enquiry (`/contact`)
**Goal:** capture leads and provide all contact channels.

Content:
1. Enquiry form (Name, Email, Phone, Service of interest, Message).
2. Direct channels: email, phone, business address/hours if applicable.
3. Expected response time.
4. Reassurance for deadline-driven users (objection/opposition/patent deadlines) to reach out urgently.

---

## FAQ (global) (`/faq`)
**Goal:** answer cross-service common questions; SEO long-tail.

Content:
1. General IPR questions (difference between trademark/copyright/patent; is registration mandatory; how to choose).
2. Process/engagement questions (how it works, timelines, what you need).
3. Links to each service's own FAQ for depth.
4. FAQPage schema.

---

## Resources / Blog (`/resources`)
**Goal:** top-of-funnel SEO + GEO; feed users into service pages.

Content:
1. Index of educational articles (each targets a specific search/AI query).
2. Suggested starter articles: "Trademark vs Copyright vs Patent — which do you need?", "How long does trademark registration take in India?", "What is a trademark objection and how do you reply?", "Provisional vs complete patent application", "Do I need to register copyright if it's automatic?".
3. Every article links to the relevant service page (conversion bridge).
4. Article schema per post.

---

## Enquiry Confirmation (`/enquiry/thank-you`)
See 03-USER-EXPERIENCE.md. Confirm receipt, restate service, state next steps + response window, offer Resources/FAQ while waiting, give urgent contact channel.

---

## Legal utility pages (`/legal/*`)
- **Site Privacy Policy** — the website's own data-handling policy (distinct from the service sold).
- **Terms of Service.**
- **Refund & Cancellation Policy.**
- **Disclaimer** — the full version of the site-wide disclaimer line.

All are standard legal/utility content; keep reachable from the footer.

---

## 404 (`/404`)
Friendly not-found message + links to the four services and search. Never a dead end.
