# Sitemap — Page Inventory

Every page below must exist and be reachable within 2 clicks of the homepage. URLs are the canonical slug for each page; keep them stable (they carry SEO value).

## Top-level pages

| Page | URL slug | Purpose | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | `/` | Orient the visitor, route them to the right service, build trust | Critical |
| Services (hub) | `/services` | Overview of all four service categories, gateway to each | Critical |
| About | `/about` | Who we are, credentials, why trust us | High |
| How It Works | `/how-it-works` | The generic engagement journey from enquiry to delivery | High |
| Contact / Enquiry | `/contact` | Primary lead capture + all contact channels | Critical |
| FAQ (global) | `/faq` | Cross-service common questions | Medium |
| Resources / Blog | `/resources` | Educational articles for SEO + GEO | High |
| Pricing | `/pricing` | Transparent cost drivers across services | Medium |
