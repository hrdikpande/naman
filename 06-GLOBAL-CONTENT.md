# Global Content & Shared Elements

Content that appears site-wide or is reused. No design.

## Site-wide legal disclaimer (required in footer on every page)
A plain-language line stating that:
- The site provides IPR filing and facilitation services, not legal representation/advice unless explicitly engaged.
- Registration outcomes depend on the relevant government authority (IP India, Copyright Office, Patent Office) and cannot be guaranteed.
- Government fees and statutory timelines are indicative and subject to change.

Keep the wording reviewed by the business; the requirement is that it is present and non-contradictory with page content.

## Global header (see 02-NAVIGATION.md)
Home · Services (dropdown) · How It Works · Pricing · Resources · About · Contact.

## Global footer
- Four link columns: Services / Company / Resources / Legal.
- Business name, email, phone.
- Legal disclaimer line.
- Copyright notice for the site itself.

## Primary action — consistent naming
Use one consistent label for the main conversion action across the whole site (e.g. "Start Enquiry" / "Get Started"). Pick one and never vary it. It always pre-tags the relevant service when opened from a service page.

## Secondary action
A lower-commitment option on educational pages: "Request a callback" / "Ask a question." Consistent naming site-wide.

## Trust elements (reused, placement flexible)
- Credentials / experience statement (sourced from About).
- "What happens after you enquire" summary.
- Honest, no-guarantee language about government outcomes.

## Reusable content blocks
- **Enquiry form block** — Name, Email, Phone, Service of interest, Message. Reused on service pages and Contact.
- **FAQ block** — reused per page with page-specific questions; FAQPage schema.
- **"Which service do I need?" mapping** — from 02-NAVIGATION.md; reused on Home and Services hub.
- **Fees/timelines values** — stored centrally as maintainable variables, injected into pages, flagged for pre-launch verification.

## Tone & voice (site-wide)
- Plain, reassuring, expert-but-accessible.
- Second person ("you"), first person plural for the business ("we").
- Define legal terms on first use.
- Never over-promise; be transparent about government-dependent steps.

## Maintainability flags
Every fee, government form number, and statutory deadline in content is a **verify-before-launch** item. Store these centrally so a single update propagates everywhere. Current known values live in the per-service content files and must be checked against live IP India / Copyright Office / Patent Office sources.
