# Page Content — Copyright Registration (`/services/copyright`)

Uses the shared service-page template.

## 1. Definition
Copyright protects your original creative work — writing, music, art, film, sound recordings, and software — giving you exclusive control over how it's reproduced, distributed, displayed, and commercialised.

## 2. Primary action
Start your copyright enquiry. (Pre-tag service = Copyright.)

## 3. Who needs it
- You're a writer, musician, artist, designer, filmmaker, or software developer.
- You want provable proof of ownership and creation date.
- You license, sell, or want to enforce rights over your work.
- You want a stronger position against plagiarism or piracy.

## 4. What's included
- Guidance on the correct work category.
- Preparation and filing of the application (Form XIV).
- Handling of objections/clarifications during examination (scope depends on package).
- Tracking through to the registration certificate.

## 5. Key facts
- **Governing law:** Copyright Act, 1957 and Copyright Rules, 2013; processed via copyright.gov.in.
- **Protection:** arises automatically on creation; registration adds legal proof and easier enforcement (not mandatory but strongly advised).
- **Typical timeline:** ~2–4 months.
- **Cost driver:** work type/category. Fees roughly ₹500–₹5,000 per work depending on category. *Verify before publishing.*

## 6. Categories of work
Six protected categories: **literary** (including software source code), **dramatic**, **musical**, **artistic**, **cinematograph films**, and **sound recordings**. Examples — literary: novels, articles, blogs, software, training material; dramatic: scripts, plays, choreography; musical: compositions and notation; artistic: paintings, drawings, photographs, architectural designs, logos.

## 7. The process
1. **Draft the application** using Form XIV with a statement of particulars.
2. **Complete details** — title, author/creator name, creation date, brief description; attach copies of the work.
3. **Pay the fee** (varies by work type).
4. **Submit** online via the e-filing portal or by post to the Copyright Office, Delhi; a **diary number** is issued on receipt.
5. **30-day mandatory waiting period** for objections before the application proceeds.
6. **Examination** — the office reviews and may issue objections or request clarifications.
7. **Certificate** — on successful registration, a copyright registration certificate is issued as proof of ownership.

## 8. Documents required
- Two copies of the work.
- Proof of identity (Aadhaar, Passport, PAN).
- Proof of ownership (NOC, assignment deed, or publishing agreement).
- Power of Attorney (if filing through an agent).
- For published works: NOC from the publisher.
- Where a person's photograph appears: NOC from that person.

## 9. Timeline detail
Typically 2–4 months end to end: Form XIV filing → diary number → 30-day objection period → examination → certificate. Some categories (e.g. software source code, artistic works tied to goods) can take longer if additional documents are needed.
